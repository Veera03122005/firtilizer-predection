# Glossary of fertilizer terms

## Terms

- **Basal dose:** fertilizer applied at or just before sowing or transplanting.
- **Top dressing:** fertilizer applied to the standing crop after sowing, usually urea.
- **Split application:** dividing a nutrient (mainly nitrogen) into two or more doses at different growth stages.
- **Recommended dose (RD):** the general amount of N, P₂O₅ and K₂O (kg/ha) advised for a crop.
- **kg/ha:** kilograms per hectare. 1 hectare = 2.47 acres; 1 acre = 0.4047 hectare.
- **N, P₂O₅, K₂O:** the way nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium are expressed in fertilizer grades and recommendations.
- **Grade (e.g., 10-26-26):** percentages of N, P₂O₅ and K₂O in the fertilizer, in that order.
- **Complex fertilizer:** a fertilizer with two or more nutrients in every granule (e.g., 17-17-17).
- **Straight fertilizer:** a fertilizer supplying mainly one nutrient (urea, MOP, SSP).
- **Crown root initiation (CRI):** the wheat stage 20–25 days after sowing when crown roots form; critical for irrigation and nitrogen.
- **Tillering:** formation of side shoots in cereals like rice and wheat.
- **Panicle initiation:** the start of ear (panicle) formation in rice, about 45–50 days after transplanting for medium-duration varieties.
- **Pegging:** the groundnut stage when flower stalks (pegs) enter the soil to form pods.
- **Leaching:** loss of nutrients carried down below the root zone by water.
- **Volatilisation:** loss of nitrogen from urea to the air as ammonia gas.
- **Biofertilizer:** living microbes that add or release nutrients (Rhizobium, Azospirillum, PSB).
- **FYM:** farmyard manure.
- **KVK:** Krishi Vigyan Kendra, a district agricultural science centre that advises farmers.
- **MRP:** maximum retail price printed on the bag.
