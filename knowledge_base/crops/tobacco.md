# Tobacco — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

A general reference used by the app for tobacco is **60 kg N, 60 kg P₂O₅ and 60 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 24.3 kg each per acre). Tobacco recommendations depend strongly on the type (for example flue-cured Virginia, bidi, chewing tobacco) and the region, and are set by tobacco research stations and boards. Treat these numbers as indicative only.

## Chloride must be avoided

Chloride reduces the burning quality and grade of tobacco leaf. For this reason the app supplies potassium as **sulphate of potash (SOP)** instead of muriate of potash (MOP) for tobacco, and the dosage table shows a note. Avoid other chloride-containing inputs and irrigation water high in chloride where possible.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at planting | 50% | 100% | 100% |
| 25–30 days after planting | 50% | — | — |

## Tips

- Excess or late nitrogen gives thick, dark leaves with poor curing quality; follow the crop-type recommendation closely.
- Potassium improves leaf quality and burning; do not skip it.
