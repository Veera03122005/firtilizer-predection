# Oil seeds (mustard and similar) — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

The app's "Oil seeds" crop uses rapeseed–mustard as the reference. A general recommendation for irrigated mustard is **80 kg N, 40 kg P₂O₅ and 40 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 32.4 : 16.2 : 16.2 kg per acre), plus **20–40 kg sulphur per hectare**. Sunflower, sesame and soybean have their own state recommendations; groundnut has a separate guide.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at sowing | 50% | 100% | 100% |
| First irrigation, 25–30 days after sowing | 50% | — | — |

## Sulphur — the key extra nutrient

Oilseeds need sulphur to form oil. Sulphur deficiency shows as pale yellow young leaves and lower oil content. Choose sulphur-containing products: single super phosphate (SSP), 20-20-0-13 (ammonium phosphate sulphate), gypsum or ammonium sulphate.

## Tips

- Under rainfed conditions, apply all fertilizer at sowing.
- Avoid excess nitrogen: it delays flowering and increases aphid problems in mustard.
