# Maize — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

General recommendation for irrigated maize: **120 kg N, 60 kg P₂O₅ and 40 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 48.6 : 24.3 : 16.2 kg per acre). High-yielding hybrids are often given more nitrogen and potassium; follow your state's hybrid recommendation and soil test.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at sowing | about one third | 100% | 100% |
| Knee-high stage, 25–30 days after sowing | about one third | — | — |
| Tasselling, 50–55 days after sowing | about one third | — | — |

Maize takes up nitrogen fastest between knee-high and silking, so the top dressings matter more than the basal dose. Place urea in a band beside the rows and earth up or irrigate after application.

## Zinc

Maize is very sensitive to zinc deficiency. Symptoms ("white bud"): broad white or pale yellow bands on young leaves near the base, short internodes. Apply zinc sulphate at about 25 kg/ha at sowing on deficient soils.

## Tips

- Farmyard manure or compost before sowing improves response to fertilizer.
- Azospirillum and phosphate-solubilising bacteria (PSB) seed treatment can reduce fertilizer needs slightly.
- Avoid waterlogging after top dressing; maize roots suffer and nitrogen is lost.
