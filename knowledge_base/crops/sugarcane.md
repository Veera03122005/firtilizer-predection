# Sugarcane — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

Sugarcane is a long-duration, heavy-feeding crop. A common recommendation for irrigated plant cane is **250 kg N, 115 kg P₂O₅ and 115 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 101 : 46.5 : 46.5 kg per acre). Recommendations vary widely by state, variety and yield target, so check the local sugarcane research station's advice.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at planting | none as urea (only N from basal products) | 100% | 100% |
| 30 days after planting | about one third | — | — |
| 60 days after planting | about one third | — | — |
| 90 days after planting | about one third | — | — |

Phosphorus and potassium go in the furrows at planting. Nitrogen is given as top dressing in three splits, completed by about 90–120 days; late nitrogen lowers juice quality.

## Example from the app

For 1 acre with 10-26-26 recommended, the app gives about 179 kg of 10-26-26 at planting and about 181 kg of urea split into roughly 60 kg at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Ratoon crop

Ratoons are often given about 25% more nitrogen than the plant crop, applied earlier, because the stubble starts growing immediately.

## Tips

- Trash mulching (spreading dry cane leaves between rows) conserves moisture and returns potassium to the soil.
- Press mud and compost improve soil organic matter.
- Apply urea in a band near the cane rows and cover with soil, then irrigate.
