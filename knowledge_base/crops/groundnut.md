# Ground Nuts (Groundnut) — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

Groundnut is a legume and fixes part of its own nitrogen. A general recommendation is **20 kg N, 40 kg P₂O₅ and 40 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 8.1 : 16.2 : 16.2 kg per acre). Recommendations differ between rainfed and irrigated crops and between states.

In the app, the model label is "Ground Nuts".

## Application schedule

All N, P and K are applied as a basal dose at sowing, placed in furrows and covered with soil.

## Gypsum — essential for pod filling

Groundnut pods take calcium directly from the soil around them. Apply **gypsum at 400–500 kg/ha at flowering / pegging, about 40–45 days after sowing**, near the base of the plants, then earth up. Gypsum also supplies sulphur, which improves oil content. Without enough calcium, pods are empty ("pops") or poorly filled.

## Tips

- Single super phosphate (SSP) or 20-20-0-13 are good phosphorus sources because they also supply sulphur and calcium.
- Rhizobium and PSB seed treatment are recommended.
- Do not disturb the soil after pegging begins, apart from the gypsum application and earthing up.
