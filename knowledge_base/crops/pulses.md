# Pulses — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

Pulses (red gram, green gram, black gram, chickpea, lentil) fix their own nitrogen from the air through root nodules, so they need only a small "starter" dose of nitrogen. A general recommendation is **20 kg N, 50 kg P₂O₅ and 20 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 8.1 : 20.2 : 8.1 kg per acre), plus about 20 kg sulphur per hectare. Phosphorus is the most important nutrient for pulses: it drives root growth, nodulation and pod setting.

## Application schedule

All fertilizer is applied as a basal dose at sowing, placed in furrows below the seed. No urea top dressing is normally needed.

## Products that fit

- 14-35-14 at about 143 kg/ha supplies almost exactly 20-50-20.
- DAP at about 110 kg/ha supplies 20 kg N and 50 kg P₂O₅; add MOP for potassium.
- Single super phosphate (SSP) or 20-20-0-13 also supply sulphur.

## Biofertilizers and sprays

- Treat seed with Rhizobium culture specific to the pulse, plus phosphate-solubilising bacteria (PSB), just before sowing. Treat with fungicide first if required, then the cultures.
- A 2% DAP foliar spray at flowering, and again about 15 days later, is commonly recommended to improve pod setting.

## Common mistakes

- Giving pulses heavy nitrogen like cereals: it suppresses nodulation and wastes money.
- Skipping phosphorus and sulphur: poor nodulation and low yield.
