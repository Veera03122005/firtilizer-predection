# Barley — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

General recommendation for irrigated barley: **60 kg N, 30 kg P₂O₅ and 20 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 24.3 : 12.1 : 8.1 kg per acre). Rainfed barley is usually given less, often all at sowing. Malting barley is given less nitrogen to keep grain protein low.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at sowing | 50% | 100% | 100% |
| First irrigation, 25–30 days after sowing | 50% | — | — |

## Tips

- Barley tolerates saline and alkaline soils better than wheat, but still responds to zinc on deficient soils.
- Too much nitrogen causes lodging, to which barley is prone.
