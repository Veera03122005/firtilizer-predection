# Cotton — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

General recommendation for irrigated cotton: **100 kg N, 50 kg P₂O₅ and 50 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 40.5 : 20.2 : 20.2 kg per acre). Rainfed cotton needs less; irrigated Bt hybrids are often given more. Follow your state's recommendation and soil test.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at sowing | about one third | 100% | 100% |
| Squaring, 40–45 days after sowing | about one third | — | — |
| Flowering, 70–75 days after sowing | about one third | — | — |

On light soils, potassium can be split between basal and squaring.

## Foliar sprays

- A 2% DAP spray at flowering and boll development is commonly recommended to reduce shedding of squares and bolls.
- Reddening of leaves ("red leaf") in cotton is often linked to magnesium deficiency or stress; a 1% magnesium sulphate spray is a common corrective. Confirm the cause with your local agriculture officer.

## Tips

- Black cotton soils are usually rich in potassium but low in nitrogen and phosphorus; a soil test may allow a lower potash dose.
- Excess nitrogen causes excessive vegetative growth, more sucking pests and delayed boll opening.
- Do not top dress after about 90 days; late nitrogen delays maturity.
