# Millets — fertilizer guide

## Nutrient requirement

The app's "Millets" crop covers small millets such as finger millet (ragi) and pearl millet (bajra). A general recommendation under irrigation is **60 kg N, 30 kg P₂O₅ and 30 kg K₂O per hectare** (about 24.3 : 12.1 : 12.1 kg per acre). Rainfed millets are usually given about half of this. Sorghum (jowar) and specific millets have their own state recommendations.

## Application schedule

| Stage | Nitrogen | Phosphorus | Potassium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basal, at sowing or transplanting | 50% | 100% | 100% |
| 25–30 days after sowing | 50% | — | — |

Under rainfed conditions, give the top dressing only when the soil has enough moisture.

## Tips

- Millets respond well to farmyard manure (5–10 t/ha) applied before sowing.
- Seed treatment with Azospirillum and phosphate-solubilising bacteria (PSB) is commonly recommended.
- Millets are hardy, but nitrogen beyond the recommendation causes lodging in tall varieties.
