# SSP — Single Super Phosphate (0-16-0)

## What it is

Single super phosphate contains about 16% phosphorus as P₂O₅, together with roughly 11% sulphur and around 20% calcium. It has no nitrogen or potassium.

## When it is a good alternative to DAP

SSP is a useful alternative to DAP for oilseeds (groundnut, mustard, sunflower) and pulses, because these crops need sulphur and calcium as well as phosphorus. It also does not create the alkaline zone around the granule that DAP does.

## How to apply

Apply as a basal dose at sowing, placed in furrows or mixed into the soil, like DAP. Because it has less phosphorus per kg, about three times as much SSP is needed as DAP for the same P₂O₅ (60 kg/ha P₂O₅ ≈ 375 kg/ha SSP).

## Note on nitrogen

Unlike DAP, SSP supplies no nitrogen; if you replace DAP with SSP, increase urea to make up the nitrogen DAP would have supplied (about 0.18 kg N per kg of DAP replaced).
