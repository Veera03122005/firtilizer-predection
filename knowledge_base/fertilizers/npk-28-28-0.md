# NPK 28-28-0 complex fertilizer

## What it is

Shown as "28-28" in the app, this is a high-analysis complex fertilizer with 28% nitrogen (N) and 28% phosphorus (P₂O₅) and no potassium. Because it is concentrated, fewer bags are needed for the same nutrients than with lower-grade complexes.

## When it is recommended

28-28-0 suits soils that need nitrogen and phosphorus in roughly equal amounts while potassium is adequate. It is used as a basal fertilizer for cereals, cotton, oilseeds and vegetables.

In the app's training data, 28-28 is the answer when Nitrogen and Phosphorous are similar and moderate (Nitrogen 21–24, Phosphorous 18–28 on the dataset scale) and Potassium is 0. See "About this system" for what these dataset values mean.

## How to apply

- Apply as a basal dose at sowing, mixed into the soil or placed in bands away from the seed.
- Add MOP if the crop still needs potassium (SOP for tobacco). Give remaining nitrogen as urea top dressing according to the crop schedule.

## How much

One kilogram supplies 0.28 kg N and 0.28 kg P₂O₅. For tobacco at 60-60-60 kg/ha, the app uses about 214 kg/ha of 28-28 (supplying all the N and P) plus 120 kg/ha of SOP for potassium.
