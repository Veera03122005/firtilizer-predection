# SOP — Sulphate of Potash (0-0-50)

## What it is

Sulphate of potash (potassium sulphate) contains about 50% potassium as K₂O and about 18% sulphur, and is practically free of chloride. It costs more than muriate of potash (MOP) per kg of potassium.

## When it is used

SOP is the potassium source of choice for chloride-sensitive crops. In the app, the dosage table uses SOP instead of MOP for tobacco, because chloride lowers the burning quality and grade of tobacco leaf. SOP is also used for some fruits, vegetables and potatoes for quality reasons, and it supplies sulphur, which benefits oilseeds.

## How to apply

Apply as a basal dose mixed into the soil, like MOP. On sandy soils, split into basal and one top dressing.

## How much

One kilogram of SOP supplies 0.50 kg of K₂O and about 0.18 kg of sulphur. For example, 60 kg/ha of K₂O needs 120 kg/ha of SOP.
