# NPK 20-20-0-13 (Ammonium Phosphate Sulphate)

## What it is

Shown as "20-20" in the app, this fertilizer is usually sold as 20-20-0-13: 20% nitrogen (N), 20% phosphorus (P₂O₅), no potassium, and 13% sulphur (S). Its chemical name is ammonium phosphate sulphate. It is sold in 50 kg bags.

## When it is recommended

20-20-0-13 suits soils that need nitrogen and phosphorus in equal measure, with little potassium need. The added sulphur makes it especially useful for oilseeds (groundnut, mustard, sunflower, soybean) and pulses, which need sulphur for oil and protein formation.

In the app's training data, 20-20 is the answer when Nitrogen and Phosphorous are similar and fairly low (8–15 on the dataset scale) and Potassium is 0. See "About this system" for what these dataset values mean.

## How to apply

- Apply as a basal dose at sowing, mixed into the soil or placed in furrows away from the seed.
- If the crop needs potassium, add MOP (or SOP for chloride-sensitive crops); remaining nitrogen goes as urea top dressing.

## How much

One kilogram supplies 0.20 kg N, 0.20 kg P₂O₅ and about 0.13 kg sulphur. For wheat at 120-60-40 kg/ha, the app gives 300 kg/ha of 20-20 at sowing (60 kg N and 60 kg P₂O₅), about 67 kg/ha of MOP for potassium, and about 130 kg/ha of urea at the first irrigation.
