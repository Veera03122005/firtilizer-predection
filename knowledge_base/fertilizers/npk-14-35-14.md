# NPK 14-35-14 complex fertilizer

## What it is

14-35-14 is a granular complex fertilizer containing 14% nitrogen (N), 35% phosphorus (P₂O₅) and 14% potassium (K₂O). It is phosphorus-rich with smaller amounts of N and K, sold in 50 kg bags.

## When it is recommended

14-35-14 suits soils with a high phosphorus need and moderate need for nitrogen and potassium. It is often used as a basal fertilizer for pulses, oilseeds, groundnut, cotton and vegetables. For pulses with a 20-50-20 kg/ha requirement, about 143 kg/ha of 14-35-14 supplies all three nutrients almost exactly.

In the app's training data, 14-35-14 is the answer when Phosphorous is high (22–33 on the dataset scale), Potassium is moderate (7–10) and Nitrogen is low (5–10). See "About this system" for what these dataset values mean.

## How to apply

- Apply the full quantity as a basal dose at sowing, placed in furrows below and beside the seed, or mixed into the soil at the last ploughing.
- Avoid direct contact with seed.
- Give any remaining nitrogen as urea top dressing, as shown in the app's schedule.

## How much

One kilogram supplies 0.14 kg N, 0.35 kg P₂O₅ and 0.14 kg K₂O. The app limits 14-35-14 so that no nutrient exceeds the crop's target, then fills gaps with DAP, MOP and urea.
