# NPK 17-17-17 balanced complex fertilizer

## What it is

17-17-17 is a balanced complex fertilizer with equal amounts of nitrogen (17% N), phosphorus (17% P₂O₅) and potassium (17% K₂O) in each granule, sold in 50 kg bags.

## When it is recommended

17-17-17 suits soils where all three major nutrients are moderately low, so the crop needs a balanced supply. It is commonly used as a basal fertilizer for cereals, sugarcane, vegetables, fruit crops and plantation crops.

In the app's training data, 17-17-17 is the answer when Nitrogen, Phosphorous and Potassium are all similar and moderate (about 10–15 each on the dataset scale). See "About this system" for what these dataset values mean.

## How to apply

- Apply as a basal dose at sowing or planting, mixed into the soil or placed in bands near (not touching) the seed.
- Most crops need more nitrogen than phosphorus or potassium, so additional urea is usually given as top dressing.

## How much

One kilogram supplies 0.17 kg each of N, P₂O₅ and K₂O. The app applies 17-17-17 up to the nutrient that first reaches the crop's target (often potassium for cereals), then adds DAP for any remaining phosphorus and urea for the remaining nitrogen. For wheat at 120-60-40 kg/ha, this works out to about 235 kg/ha of 17-17-17, about 44 kg/ha of DAP and about 157 kg/ha of urea.
