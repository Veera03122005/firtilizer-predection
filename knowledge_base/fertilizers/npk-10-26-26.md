# NPK 10-26-26 complex fertilizer

## What it is

10-26-26 is a granular complex fertilizer with 10% nitrogen (N), 26% phosphorus (P₂O₅) and 26% potassium (K₂O) in every granule. It is sold in 50 kg bags. Because every granule has all three nutrients, spreading is more even than with a mixture of separate fertilizers.

## When it is recommended

10-26-26 suits soils that are low in both phosphorus and potassium while nitrogen is less limiting. It is popular for potassium-demanding crops such as sugarcane, cotton, groundnut, potato, banana and vegetables.

In the app's training data, 10-26-26 is the answer when Potassium is the highest of the three (16–19 on the dataset scale), Phosphorous is moderate (15–21) and Nitrogen is low (4–13). See "About this system" for what these dataset values mean.

## How to apply

- Apply as a basal dose at sowing or planting, in furrows or mixed into the soil, a few centimetres away from the seed.
- Because it supplies only a little nitrogen, the crop's remaining nitrogen is given as urea in split top dressings. The app's schedule shows this.

## How much

One kilogram supplies 0.10 kg N, 0.26 kg P₂O₅ and 0.26 kg K₂O. The app applies 10-26-26 up to the point where the first of these reaches the crop's target, then fills any gap with DAP, MOP and urea.

## Cautions

- Do not use it as a nitrogen top dressing; most of its value is phosphorus and potassium, which should go in at sowing.
- On black soils already rich in potassium, a soil test may show that a phosphorus-only or N-P product is enough.
