# Buying fertilizer — genuine products and fair price

## At the dealer

- Buy only from licensed dealers or cooperative societies.
- Subsidised fertilizers in India are sold through point-of-sale (PoS) machines with the buyer's identity authentication; insist on the PoS receipt.
- Check that the bag is sealed and undamaged, and that the grade (for example 10-26-26), batch number, manufacturing date and MRP are printed.
- Never pay more than the MRP printed on the bag. Ask for a bill.
- Refuse forced "tagging" of other products you did not ask for; report it to the local agriculture officer.

## Spotting doubtful fertilizer

- Genuine urea granules are uniform, white and dissolve completely in water.
- DAP granules are hard, brownish-black or grey; genuine DAP does not easily crush between fingernails.
- If you suspect fake or adulterated fertilizer, keep the bag and bill and report it to the agriculture department; samples can be tested.

## About prices in the app

The app's cost estimate uses prices configured by the app owner, with the date shown under the table. Actual prices vary by product, company and location, and non-urea prices can change between seasons. Treat the app's cost as an estimate and check with your dealer.
