# Soil testing and the Soil Health Card

## Why test the soil

A soil test shows how much nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium and other nutrients are available, plus soil pH, salinity and organic carbon. Fertilizing to a soil test avoids waste on nutrients already present and corrects real shortages. In India, the Soil Health Card scheme gives farmers a card with test results and crop-wise fertilizer advice.

## How to take a soil sample

1. Take samples after harvest and before applying fertilizer or manure.
2. Walk the field in a zig-zag and collect 10–15 small samples from 0–15 cm depth (V-shaped cut with a spade, take a thin slice).
3. Avoid bunds, channels, compost-pit spots, tree bases and recently fertilized areas.
4. Mix all samples on a clean cloth, remove stones and roots, and keep about 500 g by repeated quartering.
5. Dry in shade, put in a clean cloth bag, and label with name, survey number, crop history and date.
6. Submit to the nearest soil testing laboratory or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).

Test every two to three years, or each season for intensive crops.

## Fertility ratings used for adjusting doses

Available nutrients are usually rated Low, Medium or High. A commonly used rating table (kg/ha) is:

| Nutrient | Low | Medium | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Available nitrogen (N) | below 280 | 280–560 | above 560 |
| Available phosphorus (P₂O₅) | below 23 | 23–56 | above 56 |
| Available potassium (K₂O) | below 141 | 141–336 | above 336 |

Laboratories and states may use slightly different limits; use the ratings printed on your Soil Health Card.

## How the app uses ratings

In the predictor, you can choose Low, Medium or High for N, P and K (default Medium). The app increases the crop's recommended dose by 25% for a Low rating and reduces it by 25% for a High rating, which mirrors the common practice used with soil-test-based recommendations.

Note: the Nitrogen, Phosphorous and Potassium numbers entered for the ML prediction are on the training dataset's scale, not laboratory kg/ha values. The ratings are what adjust the dose.
