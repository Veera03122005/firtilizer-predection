About Smart Fertilizer Advisor

Smart Fertilizer Advisor combines machine learning, rule-based agronomic balance calculations, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to help farmers optimize fertilizer application and minimize input cost.

Rather than providing a single fertilizer name without context, the advisor calculates required bags and balances primary, secondary, and micronutrients with complete application split timelines.

Agronomic Limitations & Caveats
  • Public Training Dataset Scale: The model is trained on a public 99-row dataset. N/P/K inputs correspond to synthetic score indexes from the dataset rather than direct laboratory soil-test kg/ha figures.
  • Feature Importance: Cross-validation indicates that soil nutrient levels (N, P, K) carry over 75% of the predictive signal; environmental parameters contribute marginal variance.
  • Commercial Reliance: Dosage and split schedules are based on Indian state university typical recommendations. Farmers should verify current commercial fertilizer MRPs and consult local Agricultural Extension Officers before commercial purchase.
